# 100-step research and development programme

Owner-supplied runtimes remain the persistent actuators. Execute ten-step batches, report research and implementation results, and explicitly identify the next task. Each completed result receives VFS actor admission, independent byte readback and an observer receipt, plus an owner-runtime digest-bound transaction and separate journal verification. A planning document or prior historical test does not satisfy a new functional completion gate. External capability blockers remain open and do not stop independent work.

The machine-readable ledger records dependencies, status and evidence. Baseline steps first verify current rather than remembered state. Subsequent steps move through integrity/recovery, packaging, HCI, private data handling, cloud/native publication, propagation research and promotion. Production claims need their own deployment and assessment evidence.
